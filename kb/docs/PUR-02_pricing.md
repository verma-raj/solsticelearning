# List pricing

List prices in USD per seat:

| Plan | Monthly billing | Annual billing |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | $6 per seat per month | $60 per seat per year |
| Business | $9 per seat per month | $90 per seat per year |
| Enterprise | Custom quote | Custom quote |

Taxes are added at checkout. Prices in EUR and GBP are shown on the Pricing page.
