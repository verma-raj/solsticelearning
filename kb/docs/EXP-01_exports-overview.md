# Exporting data: overview

You can export reports from the Solstice Admin Console under **Reports > Export**.

- Available formats: **CSV, XLSX and PDF**
- Who can export: users with the **Admin** or **Reporting Manager** role
- Two modes: **instant exports** (downloaded straight away) and **bulk exports** (processed in the background for large data sets)

The limits for each mode are described in "Row limits by format" and "Bulk exports".
