# Passwords for single sign-on (SSO) users

If your organization uses SSO (for example Microsoft Entra ID, Okta or Google Workspace), your Solstice sign-in is controlled by your identity provider.

- The **Forgot password?** link is hidden for SSO users, because Solstice does not store a password for you.
- To change or reset your password, use your organization's identity provider or contact your IT team.
- Admins cannot send Solstice password reset emails to SSO-only users.

SSO is available on the Business and Enterprise plans.
