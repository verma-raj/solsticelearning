# Switching between monthly and annual billing

- **Monthly to annual:** you can switch at any time. The annual plan starts on your next monthly billing date.
- **Annual to monthly:** you can only switch at your annual renewal date. Make the change before the renewal date.

Switching cycles does not change your seat count or plan tier.
