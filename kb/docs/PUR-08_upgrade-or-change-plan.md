# Upgrading or changing your plan

- **Upgrade (for example Starter to Business):** takes effect immediately. You pay the price difference, prorated for the rest of the current cycle.
- **Downgrade:** takes effect at your next renewal. You keep your current plan until then.

Changes are made under Admin Console > Billing > Subscription by an Account Owner or Billing Admin. Moving to Enterprise requires contacting Sales.
