# Higher export limits on the Enterprise plan

Enterprise organizations get increased limits:

- Bulk exports of up to **5,000,000 rows** per request (instead of 1,000,000)
- Up to **100 scheduled exports**
- SFTP delivery for scheduled exports

Other limits, including the 24-month date range and the hourly instant-export limit, are the same as on other plans.
