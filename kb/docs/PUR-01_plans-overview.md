# Solstice plans overview

Solstice Learning is sold in three plans:

| Plan | Minimum seats | Includes SSO |
|---|---|---|
| Starter | 10 | No |
| Business | 25 | Yes |
| Enterprise | 250 | Yes, plus higher limits and premium support |

You pay per seat (active learner). You can start on any plan and upgrade later.
