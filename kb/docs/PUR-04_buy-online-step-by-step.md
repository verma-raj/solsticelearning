# How to purchase a subscription online

Starter and Business plans can be bought online by an **Account Owner** or **Billing Admin**.

1. Sign in and go to Admin Console > **Billing > Choose a plan** (or use **Buy now** on the Pricing page).
2. Select a plan and a billing cycle (monthly or annual).
3. Enter the number of seats.
4. Enter billing details: company name, billing address and tax ID.
5. Enter a payment method and, if you have one, a promo code.
6. Review the order and select **Place order**.

Your subscription is activated immediately and a confirmation email is sent.
