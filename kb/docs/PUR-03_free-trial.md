# Free trial

Every new organization can try Solstice free for **14 days**.

- No credit card is required.
- Up to **10 seats**.
- The trial does not convert to a paid plan automatically. You choose when to buy.
- If you do not buy, trial data is kept for **30 days** after the trial ends and then deleted.
