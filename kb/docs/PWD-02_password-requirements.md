# Password requirements

Every Solstice password must meet these rules:

- At least **12 characters** long
- At least one uppercase letter, one lowercase letter, one number and one symbol
- Must not contain your email address or username
- Must not match any of your **last 5 passwords**

By default, passwords do not expire. Organizations on the Business and Enterprise plans can turn on password expiration in Admin Console > Security and choose 60, 90 or 180 days.
