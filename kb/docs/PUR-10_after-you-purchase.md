# What happens after you buy

Once you've placed your order:

- You'll get an order confirmation email and your first invoice straight away (card orders).
- Your seats are live immediately, so you can invite learners from Admin Console > Users > Invite.
- Enterprise customers can ask for the Master Services Agreement (MSA) and Data Processing Addendum (DPA) to be sent to them.

Nothing arrived? Check with your Billing Admin. The confirmation goes to the billing contacts.
