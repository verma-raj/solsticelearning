# Account lockout after failed sign-ins

After **5 consecutive failed sign-in attempts**, a Solstice account is locked for **15 minutes**.

You can regain access in two ways:

- Wait for the 15 minutes to pass, or
- Reset your password using the emailed link. A completed reset unlocks the account immediately.

Admins can also unlock a learner under Admin Console > Users > **Locked**.

If the **Account Owner** is locked out and cannot receive reset emails, contact Solstice Support. We verify identity and typically restore access within 1 business day.
