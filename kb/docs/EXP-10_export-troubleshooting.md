# Export troubleshooting tips

**My export stops partway or says it's too large.** You've gone over the instant export row limit. Narrow your filters or switch to a bulk export.

**My download link doesn't work any more.** Links expire after 7 days. Run the export again. Your export history is kept for 30 days.

**The CSV looks garbled in Excel.** The file is UTF-8. In Excel on Windows, use Data > From Text/CSV and choose UTF-8 as the file origin instead of double-clicking the file.

**I can't find the Export button.** You need the Admin or Reporting Manager role.
