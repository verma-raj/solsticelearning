# Invoices, receipts and billing contacts

An invoice is emailed to your billing contacts on every charge date. You can also download any invoice as a PDF from Admin Console > Billing > **Invoices**.

You can list up to **3 billing contacts** who receive invoices and billing notices. Receipts for card payments are included in the same email. Only Account Owners and Billing Admins can view invoices.
