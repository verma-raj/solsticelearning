# Annual billing and renewal

On an annual plan you pay for 12 months up front.

Solstice sends renewal reminder emails to your billing contacts **30 days** and **7 days** before the renewal date. Your card is charged on the renewal date unless you have turned off auto-renew in Admin Console > Billing > **Subscription**.

Prices for an annual term are fixed for the whole term.
