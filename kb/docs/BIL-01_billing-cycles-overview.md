# Billing cycles: overview

Solstice training subscriptions are billed on either a **monthly** or an **annual** cycle, chosen at purchase.

- Your **billing anniversary is the day you purchased** the subscription.
- Monthly plans are charged on that day each month. If the day does not exist in a month (for example the 31st), you are charged on the last day of that month.
- Annual plans are charged once a year on the anniversary.
- Subscriptions renew automatically unless auto-renew is turned off.
