# Add-ons

Optional add-ons can be added at checkout or later:

- **Course Authoring Studio:** $3 per author per month, available on Business and Enterprise
- **Premium Support:** $500 per month flat (24x7 support, 1-hour response for urgent issues), available on Business. Included with Enterprise.

Add-ons are billed on the same cycle as your subscription.
