# Who is allowed to purchase?

Only users with the **Account Owner** or **Billing Admin** role can place orders, change plans or edit payment details.

Other admins who need a purchase can select **Request purchase** in Admin Console > Billing, which notifies the Account Owner and Billing Admins by email.
