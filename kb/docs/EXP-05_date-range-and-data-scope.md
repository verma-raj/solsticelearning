# Date range limits for exports

A single export can cover a maximum date range of **24 months**.

Solstice retains reporting data for **7 years**. To obtain data older than 24 months, run several exports covering consecutive ranges of up to 24 months each, or ask Solstice Support to prepare an archive export for you.
