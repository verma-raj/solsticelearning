# Windows sign-in passwords: what Solstice does and doesn't manage

Solstice Learning does **not** manage your Windows sign-in password, whether it is a local account, a Microsoft account or a domain account. Solstice support and Solstice admins cannot reset it.

To reset your Windows password, contact your IT department or use Microsoft's own account recovery options.

Solstice Desktop for Windows supports **Windows Hello** (fingerprint, face or PIN) to unlock the app after you have signed in once with your Solstice password. Windows Hello does not replace your Solstice password, and changing your Windows PIN has no effect on your Solstice account.
