# Accepted payment methods

Solstice accepts:

- **Credit and debit cards:** Visa, Mastercard and American Express (all plans, monthly or annual)
- **ACH direct debit:** US bank accounts, annual plans only
- **Purchase order with invoice (net 30):** annual Business and Enterprise plans only

Payment methods are managed under Admin Console > Billing > Payment methods.
