# Cancellation and refunds

To cancel, turn off auto-renew under Admin Console > Billing > Subscription. You keep access until the end of the period you have already paid for.

Refunds:

- **Monthly plans:** no refunds for a partial month.
- **Annual plans:** a full refund is available within **14 days of your first annual purchase**. After that, annual payments are non-refundable.
- Seat reductions are never refunded (see seat changes).
