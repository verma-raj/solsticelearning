# Bulk exports for large data sets

Use a bulk export when you need more than 50,000 rows.

1. Go to Reports > **Bulk export** and choose the report and filters.
2. Submit the request. It runs in the background, typically finishing within 30 minutes.
3. You receive an email with a download link.

Details:

- Bulk exports are **CSV only**, up to **1,000,000 rows** per request.
- Output is delivered as a **ZIP** containing CSV files of up to **250,000 rows** each.
- The download link is valid for **7 days**.
- Your organization can run up to **5 bulk exports at the same time**.
