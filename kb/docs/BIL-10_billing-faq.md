# Billing FAQ: quick answers

**When will I be charged?** On the same date each month (monthly) or the same date each year (annual). That date is the day you bought.

**Will you warn me before my annual renewal?** Yes: emails 30 and 7 days before.

**Do prices change?** If we change list prices we tell you by email at least **60 days** ahead, and the new price applies from your next renewal.

**Who gets the invoices?** Your billing contacts (max 3).

**My card was declined, now what?** We retry a few times. See "What happens if a payment fails".
