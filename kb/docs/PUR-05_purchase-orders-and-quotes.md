# Buying with a sales quote or purchase order

Enterprise plans, and annual Business plans paid by invoice, are bought through Solstice Sales.

1. Contact Sales or select **Request a quote** on the Pricing page.
2. Receive a written quote, which is valid for **30 days**.
3. Send your purchase order (PO) to the address on the quote.
4. Once the PO is approved, your subscription is activated within **1 business day**.
5. You receive an invoice with **net 30** payment terms.
