# What happens if a payment fails

If a charge fails, Solstice retries automatically on **day 3, day 5 and day 7** after the original charge date and emails your billing contacts each time.

- **Day 10:** the account becomes **read-only**. Learners can view content but admins cannot add users or courses.
- **Day 30:** the account is **suspended** and learners lose access.
- Data is kept for **90 days** after suspension, then permanently deleted.

Updating your payment method and paying the outstanding invoice restores access immediately.
