# Taxes and currency

VAT, GST or sales tax is added to your invoice based on the billing address you enter. You can enter a tax ID (for example a VAT number) in Admin Console > Billing > Billing details.

Solstice bills in **USD, EUR or GBP**. The currency is selected at purchase and cannot be changed during a term.
